Section A: Life Processes & Classification

Questions 1-10

1 Application

A student observes a seed germinating and growing into a seedling. Which THREE life processes are most clearly demonstrated during this observation?

2 Analysis

A doctor notices that three different patients all have a fever. Why is the fever considered a symptom rather than evidence?

3 True/False

Respiration only occurs when an organism is actively moving or exercising.

4 Application

A student places a potted plant in a dark cupboard for a week. The plant's leaves turn yellow and it starts to wilt. Which life process has been most directly affected by the lack of light?

5 Analysis

A robot is programmed to detect obstacles and move around them, needs battery charging (similar to eating), and can produce other robots in a factory. Why is it still NOT considered a living organism?

6 Multiple Choice

Which example demonstrates BOTH sensitivity AND movement working together?

7 Application

A medical research team is developing a new treatment for a disease. They need to collect data and test their ideas. Which STEM subjects would be MOST important for this project?

8 True/False

Bacteria are considered living organisms because they perform all seven life processes, even though they consist of only a single cell.

9 Analysis

Why do organisms need to perform reproduction even though it is not necessary for their own survival?

10 Application

A scientist observes that when they give patients with headaches a certain medicine, 90% of patients report their headache disappearing within 30 minutes. What would strengthen the evidence that the medicine actually works?

Section B: Cells, Tissues & Organisation

Questions 11-18

11 Application

If a tissue is damaged and several cells die, but the organ containing that tissue continues to function normally, what does this tell you about the relationship between cells, tissues, and organs?

12 Analysis

A person's heart stops pumping effectively. Which other organ systems would be IMMEDIATELY affected and why?

13 Application

A patient's kidneys are not functioning properly. The doctor notes that waste products are building up in the patient's blood. This demonstrates that the kidneys are responsible for:

14 True/False

The skin is considered an organ, but it cannot be part of any organ system because it covers the outside of the body.

15 Analysis

A plant is growing in soil that lacks essential minerals. Which organ would be MOST directly responsible for this problem, and what might you observe?

16 Application

A person has a condition where their stomach produces too little acid. How would this MOST likely affect digestion?

17 Analysis

Muscle tissue can contract and relax. Nerve tissue can transmit electrical signals. How do these different tissues work together in the human body?

18 Application

An athlete trains regularly, causing their muscle tissue to increase in size and strength. Which life process does this represent, and at what level of organization does it occur?

Section C: Photosynthesis & Plant Function

Questions 19-25

19 Analysis

A plant is placed in an airtight container with a burning candle. After a few hours, the candle goes out. If the plant is left in the sealed container in sunlight, what would happen and why?

20 Application

A scientist sets up an experiment with two identical plants. Plant A is given water with normal carbon dioxide levels in the air. Plant B is given water but placed in an environment with very low carbon dioxide. What would be the MOST likely result after two weeks?

21 Analysis

During photosynthesis, energy from sunlight is converted into chemical energy stored in glucose. What happens to this energy when the plant later uses the glucose?

22 True/False

Photosynthesis can occur in complete darkness as long as the plant has water and carbon dioxide.

23 Application

A gardener notices that leaves on the lower branches of a tree, which are shaded by upper branches, are larger than the leaves receiving direct sunlight on top. What is the MOST likely explanation?

24 True/False

The oxygen that humans breathe is largely produced as a waste product from photosynthesis in plants and algae.

25 Analysis

A neurologist studies a patient who has difficulty forming new memories but can still breathe, maintain heartbeat, and control body temperature. Which part of the brain is MOST likely affected, and why do other functions continue?
